# CreditVivo Follow-Up Template Pack

Created 2026-07-08.

Purpose: provide safe SMS/email templates for leads, prospects, active clients, missing documents, monthly updates, and partner referrals.

## Rules

- Do not include sensitive details in SMS.
- Do not ask for SSNs, bureau passwords, IDs, or full account numbers by ordinary SMS/email.
- Do not promise score increases, deletions, approvals, or timelines.
- Keep dispute language tied to possible inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicate, unverifiable, mixed-file, or fraud-related reporting.
- Use opt-in/consent-aware messaging.

## 15. Internal Follow-Up Cadence

Lead cadence:

- Minute 0-5: first SMS/call if consent exists.
- Day 1: email follow-up.
- Day 2: short SMS.
- Day 5: close-loop message.

Active client cadence:

- Same day: confirm document receipt.
- Weekly: status check if waiting.
- Monthly: full update.
- Any bureau/furnisher response: update within 2 business days where possible.

Partner cadence:

- Day 1: personal email.
- Day 2: call.
- Day 4: follow-up.
- Day 7: close-loop.
