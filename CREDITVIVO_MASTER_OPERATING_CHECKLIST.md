# CreditVivo Master Operating Checklist

Created 2026-07-08.

Purpose: keep CreditVivo focused on the practical business system: get leads, qualify them safely, onboard correctly, review facts, update clients, and avoid compliance mistakes.

## Current Launch Position

CreditVivo is ready for controlled demo / waitlist / free-review operations.

Do not open full paid credit-repair subscriptions, mass SMS campaigns, sensitive document uploads, or attorney-supported services until the required legal, security, contract, and vendor gates are complete.

## 1. Offer

Core promise:

> Repair what's wrong. Build what's missing. Protect what's next.

Safe public offer:

> CreditVivo helps consumers review their credit file, organize possible reporting concerns, and prepare clearer next steps toward mortgage, auto, apartment, credit, loan, employment, and funding goals.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed score increase
- Guaranteed deletions
- Guaranteed approval
- Instant boost
- Remove accurate negative items
- Secret dispute method
- Attorney representation unless actually engaged

## 2. Lead Capture

Public form should collect:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Credit goal
- Estimated score range
- Preferred contact method
- Short notes
- Consent to contact
- Referral source

Public form should not collect:

- SSN
- Full DOB
- ID images
- Credit report PDFs
- Full account numbers
- Bureau login credentials
- Bank statements
- Signatures

Lead response goal:

- Respond within 5 minutes when possible.
- Same business day at minimum.
- Classify the lead before any sales pitch.

## 3. Lead Classification

Use one primary track:

| Track | Customer Situation | Offer |
|---|---|---|
| `repair-review` | Possible inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicate, unverifiable, mixed-file, or fraud-related reporting | Report review and evidence-based action plan |
| `build-track` | Thin file, high utilization, few accounts, weak positive history | Credit habit and builder-readiness plan |
| `protect-track` | Fraud, identity theft, unknown accounts, family misuse, data breach | Identity/fraud checklist and evidence organization |
| `prepare-track` | Mortgage, auto, apartment, job, credit card, loan, funding goal | Approval-readiness roadmap |
| `attorney-review-lead` | Lawsuit, garnishment, judgment, active legal dispute, repeated verified fraud | Attorney eligibility packet, no legal promises |

## 4. Sales Call Goal

The goal of the first call is not to close hard. The goal is to diagnose:

- What does the client want?
- What is the timeline?
- What is blocking them?
- What evidence exists?
- Is this credit repair, credit building, identity protection, approval preparation, or legal escalation?
- Is CreditVivo a fit?

## 5. Onboarding Gates

Before paid or active services:

- Written service scope
- Consumer rights disclosure
- Cancellation language
- Privacy/data handling disclosure
- Fee timing reviewed for CROA/TSR/state-law risk
- Secure document upload process
- Client authorization process
- Compliance review of dispute workflow

## 6. Document Handling

Do:

- Use secure upload when available.
- Classify every file.
- Redact where practical.
- Keep audit history.
- Ask only for documents needed for the next step.

Do not:

- Ask clients to text SSNs.
- Ask for bureau passwords.
- Store sensitive credit files in public folders.
- Put client documents in GitHub.
- Build disputes from unsupported claims.

## 7. Review Workflow

Every reviewed item should be classified:

- Possible inaccurate reporting
- Possible incomplete reporting
- Possible outdated reporting
- Possible duplicate reporting
- Possible mixed-file reporting
- Possible identity theft / fraud reporting
- Possible unverifiable reporting
- Needs more evidence
- Accurate negative item; education/build strategy only

## 8. Client Updates

Every active client should always have:

- Current status
- Next action
- Due date
- Owner
- Missing documents
- Last contact date
- Next contact date
- Risk flag if needed

Monthly updates should explain what happened and what is next without promising results.

## 9. Automation Priorities

Build in this order:

1. Lead intake and admin queue
2. Workflow status tracking
3. Task owner and due dates
4. Safe SMS/email follow-up templates
5. Secure upload portal
6. Evidence-bound AI review summaries
7. Client portal
8. Partner referral tracking
9. Attorney eligibility packet builder

## 10. Revenue Priorities

Short-term money path:

1. Free review lead magnet
2. Partner referrals from mortgage, auto, realtor, rental, tax/accounting
3. Manual consultation process
4. Paid plan only after legal/contract/security review
5. Retention through monthly progress updates

## 11. Daily Founder Checklist

Every business day:

- Review new leads.
- Respond to high-intent leads.
- Send partner outreach.
- Follow up with warm prospects.
- Check active client next actions.
- Review compliance-sensitive messages before sending.
- Log objections and improve scripts.

## 12. Weekly Review

Track:

- Leads received
- Calls booked
- Calls completed
- Qualified leads
- Partner conversations
- Documents requested
- Documents received
- Clients waiting on CreditVivo
- Clients waiting on documents
- Attorney-review leads
- Compliance issues found

## 13. Blockers Before Scale

Do not scale until these are solved:

- Counsel-reviewed customer agreement
- State compliance review
- Secure upload/document vault
- SMS A2P approval
- Privacy and data retention policy
- Incident response plan
- Payment/fee timing review
- Partner/referral compliance process
