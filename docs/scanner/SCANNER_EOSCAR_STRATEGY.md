# Scanner e-OSCAR Strategy

Credit Vivo does not integrate directly with e-OSCAR.

The scanner may use e-OSCAR awareness to organize field-level dispute facts more clearly:

- Account identifiers
- Account type/classification
- Status/pay status
- Balances and amount fields
- DOFD and major delinquency dates
- Remarks/narrative codes
- Original creditor and furnisher details

If a bureau response says verified but does not address the specific issue, the scanner can recommend a draft Method of Verification or reinvestigation review packet.

All outputs are draft review data. Customer approval and admin review are required before any follow-up.
