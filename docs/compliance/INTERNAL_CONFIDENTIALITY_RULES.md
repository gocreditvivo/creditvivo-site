# Credit Vivo Internal Confidentiality Rules

Version: credit-vivo-confidentiality-starter-2026.07.03-v1

This is a starter operating policy and is not a substitute for attorney-reviewed employment, contractor, vendor, or NDA documents.

## Confidential Customer Data

Credit Vivo customer data is confidential. This includes credit reports, identity information, account numbers, documents, dispute drafts, customer messages, scanner outputs, raw evidence, workbooks, and audit logs.

No team member, contractor, or vendor may access, copy, download, share, print, email, upload, or disclose customer data unless required for an approved business purpose and authorized by Credit Vivo.

## Prohibited Actions

Do not:

- Share customer reports by personal email.
- Store customer reports in GitHub.
- Store customer reports in ordinary cloud folders.
- Upload customer reports to public AI tools.
- Send raw reports to social accounts.
- Copy SSN, DOB, or account numbers into ordinary notes.
- Download files to personal devices.
- Use customer data for training, marketing, demos, or screenshots.
- Discuss customer data publicly.

## Required Actions

Team members must:

- Use approved systems only.
- Use role-based access.
- Use MFA.
- Log access to sensitive files.
- Mask account numbers where possible.
- Report suspected data exposure immediately.
- Delete temporary files after processing.
- Keep customer data out of GitHub and non-secure cloud storage.

## Employee / Contractor Starter Acknowledgment

I understand that Credit Vivo handles sensitive customer information, including credit reports and identity-related data. I agree not to disclose, copy, misuse, or access customer information except as authorized for my work. I agree not to store customer data in personal accounts, public tools, GitHub, or unauthorized cloud folders. I will report any suspected unauthorized access or disclosure immediately.
