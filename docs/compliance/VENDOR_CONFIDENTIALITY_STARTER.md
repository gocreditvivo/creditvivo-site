# Credit Vivo Vendor Confidentiality Starter

Version: credit-vivo-vendor-confidentiality-starter-2026.07.03-v1

This is a starter clause for vendor onboarding and should be replaced or reviewed by qualified counsel before final use.

## Confidentiality Clause

Vendor agrees to keep all Credit Vivo customer information, business information, technical information, scanner outputs, workbooks, source files, account data, customer communications, and operational processes confidential.

Vendor may access such information only as necessary to perform approved services for Credit Vivo and may not disclose, sell, copy, reuse, train models on, or transfer such information without written authorization from Credit Vivo.

Vendor must promptly notify Credit Vivo of any suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse of customer information.

## Data Handling Rules

Vendor must not:

- Store Credit Vivo customer data in personal accounts.
- Upload customer data to public AI tools.
- Use customer data for model training.
- Share customer data outside approved systems.
- Retain customer files after the approved work is complete unless Credit Vivo gives written authorization.

## Security Expectations

Vendor must use MFA, least-privilege access, secure devices, and approved storage/transmission methods for any authorized Credit Vivo work.
