# CV Market/Growth AI

CV Market/Growth AI is the founder-side marketing and revenue command module for Credit Vivo. It recommends lead priorities, draft campaigns, referral opportunities, revenue attribution, and dashboard actions.

The module is draft-only by design:

- AI recommends.
- Founder approves.
- Compliance is logged.
- No automatic public posting.
- No automatic customer outreach unless approved.
- No automatic dispute letters, complaint packets, legal escalation, or e-OSCAR activity.

Primary backend module: `scanner_backend/cv_market_growth_ai.py`.
