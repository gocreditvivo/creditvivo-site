# Credit Vivo Partner Outreach Pack

Purpose: start compliant B2B referral conversations with public business contacts. This is not a consumer email list and should not be used to target people by credit score.

## Ground Rules

- Use public business emails or contact forms only.
- Send one personal note at a time before using automation.
- Do not ask partners to send private credit reports, SSNs, or declined-applicant files.
- Ask partners to share a Credit Vivo opt-in link with customers who request help.
- Keep claims focused on review, education, organization, and attorney-supported escalation when appropriate.
- Track source, date sent, reply, meeting booked, referral count, and customer opt-in consent.

## Core Offer

Credit Vivo helps everyday consumers review their credit report, understand possible reporting issues in plain English, and prepare a clearer path toward mortgage, auto, rental, credit, and loan goals.

## 7-Day Manual Launch Motion

Day 1: Send 10 personal emails to mortgage, auto, realtor, bank, and tax/accounting contacts.

Day 2: Call the highest-priority 5 and ask for the right referral/contact person.

Day 3: Send partner one-pager to anyone who replies.

Day 4: Follow up with non-replies using Follow-Up 1.

Day 5: Book partner calls and log objections.

Day 6: Improve the partner page based on objections.

Day 7: Send Follow-Up 2, then review reply rate, booked calls, referral interest, and best-performing segment.
