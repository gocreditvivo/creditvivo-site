# Credit Vivo Strategy Handoff

## Core Mission

Credit Vivo helps everyday people with below-average credit move closer to real-life approvals: auto, mortgage, apartments, loans, credit cards, employment screening, and better financial security.

## Brand Promise

**Repair what’s wrong. Build what’s missing. Protect what’s next.**

This is the strongest promise because it is emotional, broad, and product-aligned without guaranteeing a score increase, deletion, approval, or timeline.

## Target Customer

The core customer is a motivated consumer around the 580-640 FICO range who is trying to reach the next approval tier. They may have credit report errors, thin history, high utilization, collections, old setbacks, identity theft concerns, or confusion about what to fix first.

Use nicer customer language:

- Everyday people
- Working consumers
- Approval-ready customers
- People rebuilding credit
- People preparing for what’s next
- Customers moving toward a financial milestone

Avoid:

- Bad credit people
- Desperate borrowers
- Unqualified customers
- Damaged credit

## Differentiator

Credit Vivo should feel like the combination of:

- AI-powered credit analysis
- Guided credit repair workflows
- Credit-builder tools
- Identity theft support
- Attorney-supported escalation resources
- Partner-ready approval paths

Do not publicly say "Dovly + Lexington Law" unless formal partnerships exist. Safer language:

> Inspired by the best of credit technology and attorney-supported dispute strategy, Credit Vivo is building a more complete credit recovery platform.

## Offer Ladder

1. Free Scan: goal quiz, score range, report checklist, recommended next step.
2. Repair: dispute support for inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicate, unverifiable, or fraud-related information.
3. Build: credit-builder education, utilization planning, payment reminders, eligible builder products.
4. Protect: credit monitoring, identity theft guidance, fraud recovery checklists, document vault.
5. Prepare: mortgage-ready, auto-ready, apartment-ready, job-ready, and funding-ready action plans.

## Claims To Use

- Helps customers move closer to life’s next approval.
- Designed to support credit readiness.
- Helps review and address potential report errors.
- Helps organize documents and track progress.
- Supports better credit habits over time.
- Provides identity theft guidance and protection workflows.

## Claims To Avoid

- Guaranteed approval.
- Guaranteed score increase.
- Instant boost.
- Delete anything.
- Remove accurate negative information.
- $3,000 cash line.
- Fix utilization instantly.
- More advanced than every app unless independently substantiated.
