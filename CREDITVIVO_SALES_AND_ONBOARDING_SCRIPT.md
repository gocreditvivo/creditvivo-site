# CreditVivo Sales And Onboarding Script

Created 2026-07-08.

Purpose: give Tim a practical call flow that converts without overpromising. Use this for phone calls, Zoom calls, and intake consultations.

## Call Positioning

Tone: calm, diagnostic, practical.

Do not sound like a desperate credit repair closer. Sound like a credit file reviewer helping the person understand what is possible and what needs proof.

## 1. Opening

> Hi, this is Tim with CreditVivo. I saw your request come in. Before I recommend anything, I want to understand what you are trying to get approved for and what you believe is holding you back.

If they ask, "Can you fix my credit?"

> CreditVivo can help review your credit file, organize possible reporting issues, and build a step-by-step plan. If something appears inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicate, unverifiable, mixed-file, or fraud-related, we can help prepare the right next step. I cannot promise deletions, score increases, or approval results.

## 2. Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What are you trying to do: car, home, apartment, card, loan, job, or business funding?
2. When do you need it?
3. Do you know your approximate score range?
4. Have you looked at all three reports: Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion?
5. What items do you think are wrong or suspicious?
6. Do you have proof, letters, payment records, identity theft documents, or denial letters?
7. Have you disputed before?
8. Any lawsuits, judgments, garnishments, bankruptcy, or active collection threats?

## 3. Qualification

Good fit:

- Has a real goal.
- Can provide documents.
- Understands results are not guaranteed.
- Wants review and guidance, not magic.
- Has possible reporting issues or needs a build/prep plan.

Poor fit:

- Wants accurate negative items erased.
- Wants fake identity theft claims.
- Refuses to provide documents.
- Demands guaranteed score increase.
- Has active legal matter but expects non-lawyer legal advice.

## 4. Explain The Process

> The process is simple. First, we identify your goal. Second, we review the reports and documents. Third, we separate possible reporting issues from items that need credit-building strategy. Fourth, we create an action plan. If a dispute is appropriate, it must be based on facts and supporting evidence. If it looks legal, we prepare it for attorney review instead of guessing.

## 5. Set Expectations

> Credit bureaus and furnishers investigate and respond based on their records and the evidence provided. Some items may update, verify, delete, or need more review. Credit scores can also move for many reasons, including balances, utilization, new reporting, aging, inquiries, and score model differences.

## 6. Safe Close

> The next safe step is a file review. I will send you the document checklist and we will start with the information needed to understand your situation. Please do not send Social Security numbers, bureau passwords, IDs, or full account numbers by regular text or email.

## 7. Objections

### "Can you remove collections?"

> We can review whether the collection appears inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicate, unverifiable, mixed-file, or fraud-related. If it is accurate and timely, we cannot promise removal. In that case, we focus on strategy, documentation, and approval readiness.

### "How fast will my score go up?"

> I cannot promise a score increase or timeline. Scores depend on many factors, including balances, utilization, payment history, account age, new reporting, and how bureaus/furnishers respond. What we can do is organize the file and take the next best evidence-based step.

### "I need approval next month."

> Then we should focus on the items most likely to affect that approval and avoid wasting time. We will separate urgent approval-readiness steps from longer-term repair/build steps.

### "I already disputed and they verified."

> A verified response does not always mean the issue is over, but the next step needs stronger facts and documents. We should review the bureau response, the original report, and any creditor letters before deciding what to do next.

### "Can I do this myself?"

> Yes. Consumers can dispute credit report errors themselves. CreditVivo helps organize the process, review possible issues, track next steps, and prepare a clearer file when the situation is confusing or document-heavy.

## 8. Red Flag Response

If client asks for fake dispute or fake fraud claim:

> I cannot help create a claim that is not true or supported by your documents. CreditVivo only works with facts, evidence, and legitimate reporting concerns.

If client has lawsuit/garnishment:

> That may need legal review. I can help organize a packet, but I cannot give legal advice or promise a legal result.
